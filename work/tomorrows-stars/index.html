Our Why

We believe in the power of our youth

Tomorrow’s Stars Foundation was created to provide youth in the Las Vegas community with free programming focused on education, technology, athletics, and community service.

  • Free programming, every pillar
  • Mentors from media, medicine & sport
  • 50+ partners across Southern Nevada
Read our story
Six Pillars

Every kind of star

Free programming across six fields of opportunity, with the mentorship and access to go pro in whatever they love.

01

Education

In- and after-school programming, tutoring, and scholarships that turn potential into achievement.

02

Technology

Coding, game design, and digital-maker labs that build tomorrow’s innovators.

03

Athletics

Youth camps, leagues, and elite coaching that build confidence on and off the court.

04

Medicine

Hands-on exposure to sports medicine, surgery, and the health sciences for future clinicians.

05

Arts

Broadcasting, film, music, and media programs that give young creators a real stage.

06

Engineering

Design, build, and problem-solving experiences that spark the next generation of makers.
