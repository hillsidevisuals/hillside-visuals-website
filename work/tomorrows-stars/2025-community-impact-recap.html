2026-09-12

2025 Community Impact Recap

In 2025, Tomorrow’s Stars Foundation continued to uplift and empower youth across Las Vegas through arts education, media training, civic engagement, and meaningful community partnerships. What began as a small arts program has grown into a citywide force for youth development, leadership, and service.

Youth education & creative development

Performing & Fine Arts students trained in music, dance, acting, fashion design, and visual arts, building confidence, discipline, and cultural awareness. The 13-week track culminated in a final showcase featuring performances, fashion pieces, and student portfolios.

On-Air Broadcasting & Film students worked inside a live broadcast studio, writing scripts, hosting segments, filming, editing, and producing broadcast-ready content. This parallel 13-week curriculum gave students hands-on experience and professional-quality work for their creative portfolios.

Citywide performances & youth representation

TSF performers delivered monthly showcases at First Friday, the Las Vegas Civic Center, and Mountain Crest Park, and were invited to perform at both Democratic and Republican gala events.

  • National Anthem performances across March Madness events
  • TSF Choir and artists featured at the City of Las Vegas Christmas Tree Lighting
  • Over 100 student performers represented TSF during NBA Summer League
  • National Anthem Idol gave local students a platform to perform for industry professionals
  • 24 student-athletes (ages 14–18) selected and trained for the official NBA Summer League Ball Crew

Community partnerships & educational outreach

  • Continued support for CCSD through the Adopt-A-School program
  • Honoring 40+ teachers, coaches, first responders, military members, and community leaders at NBA Summer League
  • Ongoing collaboration with the Nevada SPCA, with all featured puppies successfully adopted
  • Partnership with The Sunday Collective Fashion House (NYC) to produce Summer League’s first-ever on-court fashion show
  • Collaboration with the Las Vegas Clark County Library District, including sustainability classes and a special edition NBA Summer League library card available in all 25 branches

Civic engagement & annual traditions

For more than 15 years, TSF has played a pivotal part in the Martin Luther King Parade, one of the largest in the country. Students and staff provided volunteer support and produced the televised pre-show, featuring TSF performers and local professional talent.

This year also marked a new outreach opportunity with the National Martin Luther King Commission in Little Rock, Arkansas. TSF recognized several Arkansas educators and coaches at Summer League.

Military families & workforce development

A major milestone was the creation of the first Military Internship: “Transitioning to the Private Sector.” Developed with longtime supporter Sergeant First Class Benjamin Hewitt, retiring after 20 years of service and now an Associate Professor of Military Sciences at USC, the internship offers veterans structured pathways into civilian careers.

STEM & STEAM advancement

Through our ongoing partnership with the 4th Family Foundation, TSF delivered hands-on STEM and STEAM clinics teaching engineering and science concepts through basketball. The 2025 Summer League clinic engaged more than 60 local youth.

2025 at a glance

  • Expanded arts, media, fashion, STEAM, and performance opportunities
  • Delivered citywide performance experiences across major civic events
  • Strengthened partnerships with CCSD, LVCCLD, LVMPD, military groups, and nonprofit agencies
  • Recognized educators, first responders, and community leaders
  • Created pathways for youth performers, student-athletes, and veterans

Develop talent. Uplift youth. Strengthen community.

As we look ahead, TSF invites organizations, brands, and community partners to join us in expanding youth arts, education, and leadership opportunities across Las Vegas.
