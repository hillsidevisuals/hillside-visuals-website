Tomorrow’s Stars Foundation Inspires the Next Generation at the Summer League Jr. NBA STEM Clinic

The 2025 NBA 2K26 Summer League once again proved to be more than just a showcase of basketball’s rising stars. Tomorrow Stars Foundation made it a platform for education, mentorship, and community impact.

In partnership with the 4th Family Foundation, the Tomorrow’s Stars Foundation (TSF) proudly co-hosted the Summer League Jr. NBA STEM Kids Clinic, an annual program that blends the excitement of basketball with the innovation of STEM learning.

This year’s clinic brought together 50 young athletes ages 11–14 from across the Las Vegas area for a full day of skills training, leadership development, and hands-on STEM activities. Supported by six local coaches and an incredible team of 13 staff members from the 4th Family Foundation, the clinic offered a one-of-a-kind experience that challenged participants both on and off the court.

A highlight of the day came from special guest coach Gary Chivichyan of the Milwaukee Bucks, who shared his journey from collegiate standout to professional player. His message of perseverance, discipline, and continuous learning echoed TSF’s commitment to empowering youth through education and opportunity.

Through engaging drills, teamwork exercises, and interactive STEM lessons, participants learned the principles of basketball problem-solving, and collaboration. Tomorrow Stars Foundation identifies these as key skills that drive success in science, technology, engineering, and math.

The Tomorrow’s Stars Foundation continues to expand its footprint at NBA Summer League, creating programs that unite sport, learning, and leadership. By connecting youth with positive role models and hands-on experiences, TSF is shaping the next generation of student-athletes, innovators, and community leaders.
