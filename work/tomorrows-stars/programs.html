Flagship Programs

Where the work happens

City of Las Vegas · Codee Locs

Next Move Arcade Jam

Code Your Future. Where gaming culture meets the game: youth build and play their own video games through code, then bring them to the NBA Summer League stage.

  1. 1
    The VisionIntroduce coding structure, demonstrate the software, and set the goals students will hit.
  2. 2
    The PrototypeStudents storyboard their game and build their first working prototypes with mentor support.
  3. 3
    End ScreenStudents present their finished games to a board of community-partner judges at NBA Summer League, and their games go playable on the Summer League floor.

City of Las Vegas · Arthrex · Goode Surgical

Clinical Access Program

Introduction, demonstration, execution. Hands-on exposure to sports medicine and surgical careers for aspiring young health professionals, including a Summer League Sports Medicine Series with working physicians.

  1. 1
    IntroductionA set of cadavers (shoulder, knee, and ankle) plus an intro to anatomy before students step into the lab.
  2. 2
    DemonstrationInstruction on navigating the body, the use of surgical instruments, and their real-life implications.
  3. 3
    ExecutionGuidance through real surgical implants, examining specimens, and hands-on surgical techniques.

City of Las Vegas · The Gas Station Studios

Next Move Media Academy

Training the next generation of youth storytellers across filmmaking, video, and digital media, culminating in a commercial that ran on the Summer League jumbotron.

  1. 1
    The Vision · DirectingLeading the set, framing the narrative, and bringing a creative concept to life.
  2. 2
    The Look · Camera & LightingMastering shot types, understanding how angles invoke emotion, and painting a scene with light.
  3. 3
    The Sound · Audio EngineeringRigging lavalier mics, operating boom sticks, and capturing crystal-clear audio on location.
  4. 4
    The Climax · Commercial ProductionCollaborating as a full crew to produce a high-impact, professional-grade commercial.
