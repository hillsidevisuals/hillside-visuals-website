2026 So Far: A Recap of the Year in Motion

2026 has been a year of building. Tomorrow’s Stars Foundation launched a new generation of flagship programs, expanded into technology and media career pathways, and once again put Las Vegas youth on the floor at NBA Summer League.

Here is where things stand so far.

Three new flagship programs

Our current programming is built around hands-on career exposure across three of our six areas of focus.

Next Move Arcade Jam · Technology

Students learn to build and play their own video games through code, moving from first concept to working prototype to a finished game presented before community-partner judges at NBA Summer League. Delivered with the City of Las Vegas and Codee Locs.

Next Move Media Academy · Arts

Directing, camera and lighting, audio engineering, and full commercial production, taught inside a working studio. Student work has run on the jumbotron at Summer League. Delivered with the City of Las Vegas and The Gas Station Studios.

Clinical Access Program · Medicine

Real exposure to sports medicine and surgical careers, including anatomy instruction, surgical technique, and a Summer League Sports Medicine Series alongside working physicians. Delivered with the City of Las Vegas, Arthrex, and Goode Surgical.

Youth Basketball Camp

Our two-day summer camp on July 14–15 brought elite on-court instruction to Las Vegas youth, led by Blueprint Project founder Gary Chivichyan alongside NBA coaches and special guests including Coach Mike Woodson (Sacramento Kings), Coach Jim Boylen (Indiana Pacers), and Monty Buckley.

Beyond skills and drills, the camp focused on teamwork, focus, and the kind of mentorship that carries off the court. The camp was run in partnership with the Blueprint Project and Siegel Cares, with 25% of proceeds benefiting Tomorrow’s Stars Foundation programming.

Back on the floor at Summer League

NBA Summer League remains our largest stage. TSF students returned to the Thomas & Mack floor this year across programming, performance, and service roles, with our program cohorts presenting their work directly to fans and partners.

What is ahead

We are continuing to expand access across all six areas of focus: education, technology, athletics, medicine, arts, and engineering. Every program we run is free to the young people who attend, funded entirely by donations and partnerships.

Support the work

To sponsor a program, partner with us, or enroll a student, contact our team or make a contribution. Every gift funds free seats for Las Vegas youth.
