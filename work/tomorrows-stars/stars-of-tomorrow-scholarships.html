Stars of Tomorrow Scholarships

STARS OF TOMORROW SCHOLARSHIPS

In partnership with the Public Education Foundation (PEF), TSF scholarships increased access to post secondary educational opportunities available to college-bound students residing in Nevada. TSF scholarships were intended to help students pursue higher educational goals and, in many cases, make the college and university experience accessible to students who may not have the necessary financial means.

Four (4) $1,000.00 scholarships were awarded in previous years.

Stars of Tomorrow Scholarships (fund #02-9459) were open to current Nevada high school seniors who plan to study S.T.E.M. related field (science, technology, engineering, math), a communications related field (communications, broadcasting, journalism, graphic design/media), a sports related field (sports management/marketing, athletics/physical education), a medical related field, or education at a four-year accredited college or university in Nevada.

SCHOLARSHIP TIPS VIDEO

This video was created to empower you, the student, with pertinent scholarship information and to help you achieve success when applying for the Stars of Tomorrow Scholarship, and all other scholarships that you are eligible to apply for, this year.
