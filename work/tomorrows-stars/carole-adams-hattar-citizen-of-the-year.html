Carole Adams Hattar Named Citizen of the Year by the City of Las Vegas

A highlight of our year: Carole Adams Hattar, founding member and director of Tomorrow’s Stars Foundation, was recognized as Citizen of the Year by Mayor Shelley Berkley and the City of Las Vegas.

The honor celebrates more than 25 years of Carole’s service and leadership, and her lasting impact through both Tomorrow’s Stars Foundation and NBA Summer League, where she serves as Director of Community Relations.

A quarter century of showing up

Carole has been the connective tissue between TSF and the Las Vegas community for as long as the foundation has existed. The partnerships that make our programming possible, with the Clark County School District, the Las Vegas Clark County Library District, local military groups, first responders, and dozens of nonprofit agencies, exist in large part because she built and maintained them.

Her work is also why TSF is able to honor others. In 2025 alone, the foundation recognized 40+ teachers, coaches, first responders, military members, and community leaders at NBA Summer League.

From all of us

Congratulations, Carole. This recognition reflects what everyone who has worked alongside you already knew.
